# GRC Workbench privacy policy

_Last updated: October 22, 2026_

GRC Workbench is a desktop app for tracking vulnerability remediation: it
reads vulnerability scan files, compares scan cycles, and produces tickets,
reports and audit records. It is made by an independent developer (publisher
"dmd-dev"). It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Tenable or any ticketing
vendor.

## What GRC Workbench collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no
telemetry and no tracking. The developer never receives any of your data.

## What stays on your computer

Everything you work with stays on your own computer:

- the client and site details you enter, and the app's settings;
- the scan cycles you save (findings, affected hosts, dates and trends);
- the Fix Library, ticket settings, ticket records and closure decisions;
- the report settings, including a logo if you add one;
- a log of the app's own activity, used for troubleshooting.

They are saved in the app's data folder in your user profile. Scan files are
read only when you choose them, and files the app creates (tickets, reports,
workbooks, audit records) are saved only where you choose. Uninstalling the
app removes its data folder.

Scan files and saved cycles can contain sensitive information about the
networks that were scanned, such as host names, IP addresses and
vulnerabilities. Protect them as your organization requires.

## Network use

None. GRC Workbench does not connect to the internet or to any other system.
Tickets are exported as files for you to import into your ticket system
yourself. The Microsoft Store version is updated by the Microsoft Store.

## Children

GRC Workbench is a business tool and collects no information from anyone,
including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
